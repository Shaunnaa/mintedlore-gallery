## 🌟 Overview

**MintedLore Gallery** bridges the gap between digital art and traditional museum curation. For newcomers, digital art often lacks accessible context—stories and lore are scattered across closed Discord servers and fragmented X threads. 

MintedLore solves this by providing a unified, consumer-friendly platform where creators publish rich, episodic lore attached directly to their collections, and readers can explore immersive narrative universes. By abstracting the complexities of Web3, anyone can browse and appreciate the art, while collectors can seamlessly use their on-chain NFTs as access keys to unlock exclusive VIP chapters.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **⏳ Interactive Story Timelines:** Readers explore community lore through immersive chronological timelines and visual chapter navigation.
* **🎨 Public User Profiles & NFT Showcases:** Users curate their on-chain identity, connect socials, and showcase their verified Solana NFT collections on custom vanity URLs (`/u/[username]`).
* **📖 Token-Gated Lore & VIP Chapters:** Creators publish episodic stories. Collectors connect their Solana wallets to automatically use their real on-chain NFTs as access keys to unlock exclusive content.
* **🎟️ Community Stamp Cards:** Gamified reading incentives where verified NFT holders earn collectible stamp rewards as they progress through universe lore.
* **🛠️ Creator Studio:** A streamlined publishing suite allowing verified artists and fan communities to organize chapters under parent collections with customized theme settings and VIP thresholds.
* **🔍 Universal Search & Discovery:** Global, filterable search across collections, games, and individual stories with zero layout shift.
* **📊 Data-Driven Admin Dashboard:** Built-in analytics engine tracking platform pageviews, top-performing lore, and daily engagement trends powered by Recharts.
* **⚡ High-Performance Architecture:** Built with Next.js 15 Server Components and Framer Motion scroll animations for instantaneous load times and buttery-smooth responsiveness.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Integrations

* **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS
* **Animations & Visuals:** Framer Motion (`ScrollReveal`, `StaggerGroup`)
* **Analytics Visualizations:** Recharts
* **Backend & Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL), Service Role APIs
* **Web3 Infrastructure:**
  * `@solana/web3.js` & `@solana/wallet-adapter` (Phantom, Solflare on Mainnet)
  * **Helius DAS API:** High-speed indexed asset queries (`getAssetsByOwner`) for instant NFT ownership verification
  * **Magic Eden API:** Official collection metadata and image resolution
* **Deployment:** Vercel Edge Network
